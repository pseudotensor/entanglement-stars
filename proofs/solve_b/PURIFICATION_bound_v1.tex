\paragraph{Exact bound.}
Let the complete regular native operator \(h\), including its interior
and actual HA fields, be held fixed.  For a pure number-conserving
Gaussian physical state \(C(t)=e^{-iht}C_0e^{iht}\), use a complete
geometric split into the exterior \(X\) beyond the collar and the
finite interior \(Y\), with \(Q=\Pi_Y\) of rank \(d\).
Let \(P_g\) be the held Killing-ground projector with declared
zero-mode filling, not the continued spectral subtraction sheet.
In nats, put \(s(x)=-x\ln x-(1-x)\ln(1-x)\) and
\(F_d(z)=d\,s(\min\{z/d,1/2\})\).  Then
\[
 \left|S_X(C(t))-S_X(P_g)\right|
 \le F_d\!\left(\|Q(C(t)-P_g)Q\|_1\right)
 \le F_d\!\left(\Tr Qe^{-iht}|C_0-P_g|e^{iht}Q\right).
\]
The finite-interior entropy formula and the binary-entropy continuity
bound prove this exactly.  On an infinite parent, a compact covariance
disturbance supported in the absolutely continuous sector makes the
right side tend to zero by local spectral decay, with no rate.
Bound-sector deviations and coherences leave an explicit remainder;
singular-continuous content needs separate control.  A finite closed
parent does not have this asymptotic scattering limit.

\paragraph{What a finite return window additionally needs.}
Assume that completed correlations are side-local relative to the held
vacuum, and the unfinished joint factor acts on only \(r_X,r_Y\)
modes on the two sides.  If its pure Gaussian comparison differs from
the actual interior covariance by at most \(\epsilon\) in trace norm,
\[
 \left|S_X(C(t))-S_X(P_g)\right|
 \le 2\min(r_X,r_Y)\ln2+F_d(\epsilon).
\]
The factor two follows from the range of conditional entropy; vacuum
entanglement cannot be discarded.  If an actual common-history return
bound \(\tau_*\) restricts this unfinished factor to packets first
crossing in \((t-\tau_*,t]\), it becomes a window bound.  Completion
must hold at the observation time, including interleaving and recrossings,
not merely at an earlier first return.  In the stronger isolated
independent-pair realization, require constant unfinished Schmidt
weights, side-product reference pair blocks in the same current cut
basis, and unchanged remainder entropy.  Then each block contributes
\(s(\nu_j)\) to the excess, and the bound is the entropy content
\(\sum_{s_j\in(t-\tau_*,t]}s(\nu_j)\), plus controlled tails and
bound-sector terms.  Otherwise subtract the reference pair entropies
\(s(\nu_j^g)\) and retain the remainder's change; the nonnegative
pair-sum identity need not hold.  Its rate is in nats per infinity-clock time.
It is not the signed energy flux or an expected particle count:
\(s(\nu)/\nu\) is unbounded as \(\nu\to0\).  Converting energy
to entropy requires a positive once-counted allocation, a positive
frequency lower bound and a controlled number of modes.

\paragraph{Paper-usable conclusion and present applicability.}
Complete unit-flux one-ended scattering with only incoming vacuum
gives no stationary excitation supply.  It does not bound a transient
return time; absolute exterior entropy also contains the nonzero held
vacuum baseline.  The paper's
\(2r_s\ln(1/(a\omega))+O(r_s)\) is the maintained vacuum-annulus
characteristic comparison, not a joint quantum return certificate.
Its use as a window needs actual interior residence, channel/frequency,
packet-tail, cut and boundary controls.  The supplied collapse has a
changing complete operator even where its exterior is nearly stationary;
the separate held-field records establish neither that joint return
bound nor an infinite-exterior bound-state decomposition.  Thus the
conditional theorem applies as stated, without implying an area-scale
entropy limit or a purification deadline on that history.  Physical
entropy, the continued spectral source subtraction and nonlinear
boundary charge remain distinct.
