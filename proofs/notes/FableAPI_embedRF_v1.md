## 1. The rejected items

**1.1 "$Q_R=1$ exactly when $N_R=\beta_R=1$ and $R=2GE$." Conceded.**
From `eq:dr-charge` at an inward-branch root, $w_R=-1$, $\mathcal M(R)=R/2G$, and $\mathcal M'_R=1/2G-Rw'_R/G$, so $w'_R=\beta_R/2R$ with $\beta_R=1-2G\mathcal M'_R=1-8\pi GR^2(\rho_R-J_R)$, and $\kappa_R=N_Rw'_R$. With $\kappa_H=1/2r_E$,
$$Q_R=\frac{\kappa_R}{\kappa_H}=\frac{N_R\beta_R}{1-f_{\rm ext}},\qquad f_{\rm ext}=1-\frac R{r_E}.$$
So $Q_R=1$ iff $N_R\beta_R=1-f_{\rm ext}$. A source-free exterior from $R$ to the charge boundary is sufficient ($\mathcal M'=0$ gives $R=r_E$; $\rho_R=J_R=0$ gives $\beta_R=1$; $J=0$ outside gives $N_R=1$), not necessary; the checker's example ($N_R=1$, $\beta_R=0.8$, $R=0.8r_E$) is consistent with the rows. One conditional remark, not a claim: for positive band-diagonal heavy matter only, $\beta_R\le1$ (by $|q|\le\varepsilon$, Appendix B), and for a lagging current outside $R$, $N_R\le1$, so $Q_R\in(0,1/(1-f_{\rm ext})]$; the reported 1.04 at $f_{\rm ext}=0.04$ would then be $N_R\beta_R\simeq1$ with $\kappa_R>\kappa_H$ only because $R<r_E$. That is a candidate reading for the audit, not a result.

**1.2 $f_{\rm ext}$ as a matter fraction, and "$N_R<1$ inside matter." Conceded.**
$f_{\rm ext}E=E-\mathcal M(R)=\int_R^\infty4\pi r^2(\rho_\ell+wJ_{\ell r})\,dr$ is the net generator charge outside the root, signed, with the propagated sea's departure and the reference response inside it ([00] assumes neither positive $H_{\rm no}$ nor positive site energies). It locates no particles. For the clock, `eq:dr-radial` with $N(\infty)=1$ gives
$$\ln N_R=-4\pi G\int_R^\infty\frac{J_{\ell r}}{\mathsf k}\,dr,\qquad \mathsf k=(2G\mathcal M/r^3)^{1/2},$$
so $N_R<1$ exactly when the weighted current integral outside $R$ is positive. The computed cloud, released from coordinate rest, lags the inward flow ($\dot x_r=Nq_r/\varepsilon+v_r=0$ at release forces $q_r>0$), hence $J_{\ell r}>0$ and a clock that falls inward; matter leading the flow would raise $N_R$ above one. Presence of matter fixes no sign.

**1.3 $Q_R=-0.31$ as "noise or a non-simple root"; $Q_R\le1$. Conceded.**
An exact multiple root has zero slope and zero $\beta_R$, not a negative slope. At a simple outer root of a trapped interval $F$ passes from negative to positive, so $\kappa_R>0$, $\beta_R>0$, $Q_R>0$. The first candidate for $-0.31$ is an inner root (the inner edge of a trapped interval, where $F$ passes from positive to negative and $\beta_R<0$); [00] reports inner trapping radii in the five-slice test. Mixed fine-grid and back-averaged fields, inconsistent interpolants, derivative error at a nearly degenerate root, and constraint residuals are the other candidates. No upper bound: $Q_R>1$ whenever $N_R\beta_R>1-f_{\rm ext}$, including $N_R=\beta_R=1$ with any $f_{\rm ext}>0$. "Between 0 and 1.0 within the noise" is withdrawn.

**1.4 The fountain chronology (17.4 / 18.4). Conceded.**
The support argument stands conditionally: on a slice with $N>0$, a regular inward branch, $E>0$, and all averaged source charge inside $R_b<r_E$, every $r\in(R_b,r_E)$ has $w^2=r_E/r>1$, so a trapped interval remains; closure therefore needs net source support reaching $2GE$ (with a tail bound for the kernel). It says nothing about where that charge was earlier, whether it crossed outward, or whether it returns. [00] itself prints "every step's net transport through its old fine-grid trapping radius is inward", and a shrinking root raises $f_{\rm ext}$ with no outward transport at all. The clocks 17.4 and 18.4 belong to the figure's separate production history, and the text establishes no closing time. They cannot be promoted or transferred to either two-field size.

**1.5 "$13.2\pm0.3$" for $\eta_E\in[0.85,1]$. Conceded.**
With release at $4r_{\rm nom}$ and $r_E=\eta_Er_{\rm nom}$, $x_0=4/\eta_E$ and $T_c/r_{\rm nom}=\eta_E\,I(4/\eta_E)$, with
$$I(x_0)=(x_0+2)\sqrt{x_0-1}\,\arctan\sqrt{x_0-1}+(\sqrt{x_0}-1)^2+2\ln\bigl(1+x_0^{-1/2}\bigr),$$
which runs from 12.694 ($\eta_E=1$) through 13.225 ($\eta_E=59.05/64$) to 13.786 ($\eta_E=0.85$). The interval 12.9 to 13.5 was wrong. Beyond the number: [00] imposes no coordinate rest, so this is a comparison outer boundary's clock, not a trajectory of either cloud, and it does not carry to size 128. The marginally bound comparison's interior law $T_\pi-t_c=\tfrac23r_E[1-(R_\pi/r_E)^{3/2}]$ belongs to $e=1$ dust and must not be appended to the $e<1$ coordinate-rest trajectory, whose interior clock differs.

**1.6 The lattice-stage reconstruction. Conceded on all four parts.**
(a) $R_\pi$ is the cold flat-slice WKB comparison radius, "neither an endpoint nor an observed radius" ([00]); 10.5$a$ (isotropic, $N_B=640$) and 20$a$ (five-column bound) are two comparison constructions, and the size-128 range needed a preparation-scaling assumption [00] does not supply. (b) The momentum reading combined the width-8 run's $R_{50}$ minimum at 14 with energies read at 15 and 16 on another run; the $\sigma/a=4$ history has no slice at 13 to 14; and an average energy per particle is not a momentum. Even a monoenergetic axial chain mode would invert as $ak=\arcsin\sqrt{(a\varepsilon)^2-(am)^2}$, 0.53 to 0.71 for $a\varepsilon=0.54$ to 0.68, before momentum spread and the channel term $k/r$; all below $\pi/4$, consistent with "the dust fills to $\pi/4$ on no stored slice". (c) Reversed advection is kinematics only: for $ak\in(\pi/4,\pi/2)$, $q_r>0$ and $\cos(2ak)<0$ make both terms of `eq:dr-comoving` outward, but [00] forbids reading either $\pi/4$ bin as outgoing content. (d) Fermi pressure as the cause of recession is unsupported and contradicts `eq:C-rate`: $\partial_t\mathcal M|_R=8\pi Nr^2\int_pf(\varepsilon-q_r)^2/\varepsilon>0$ for every positive band-diagonal filling, kinetic pressure included, and with $\beta_R\dot R=2G\,\partial_t\mathcal M|_R$ it gives $\dot R>0$ at a simple outer root. Observed recession must come from finite-zone transport weights, the reference response, the light field's signed source, or other defects, none measured.

**1.7 The two-percent reference floor. Conceded.**
The number was the longitudinal second-gradient energy of the exterior flow: with $v=-(r_E/r)^{1/2}$, $(v')^2=r_E/4r^3$, and $\int_{r_E}^{4r_E}4\pi r^2\widehat\gamma_{11}a^{-2}(v')^2dr/E=2\pi\widehat\gamma_{11}g\ln4\simeq0.021$. It is not a floor: $W_2$ is subtracted in the reference by construction, and [00] reports the stored fixed reference projector's departure to be "almost exactly" that polarization; the spherical flow's $W_2=-\tfrac14\int F\!:\!\mathsf T\!:\!F/N$ carries radial and transverse strains through the full tensor, not $\widehat\gamma_{11}$ alone; and what can remain in the sources is the propagated sea's non-adiabatic departure, which $\widehat\gamma_{11}$ does not give. The exterior charge must be decomposed, not assigned.

**1.8 The $J$-sign outgoing/infalling split. Conceded.**
In the continuum a packet moves outward in the lattice frame iff $q_r/\varepsilon>|w|$, so positive relative momentum still falls inward wherever $q_r/\varepsilon<|w|$, and nothing continuum moves outward inside the root; at finite $ak$ the $\cos(2ak)$ weight can reverse this; the reference terms have no packet direction; the integrand $\rho_\ell+wJ_{\ell r}$ is signed, the bins need not be nonnegative, the $J=0$ set is dropped, and species and reference parts can cancel before the sign is taken. At most these are signed-current source bins. Direction is a momentum-resolved statement (the transport velocity, or the Killing sort [00] already uses); transport is the bond flux through fixed surfaces with the moving-root sweep separated.

**1.9 The three pass criteria and their thresholds. Conceded.** None of the three classifications follows from a small minimum, a monotone rise, or a two-thirds bin; 0.10, 0.20, and two-thirds are conventions.

**1.10 "Disagreement between the two $\beta_R$ evaluations means a non-simple root." Conceded.** Both vanish at a multiple root; disagreement diagnoses inconsistency or unresolved error. Simplicity requires a derivative bound excluding zero; a bound containing zero means "not verified".

**1.11 Zero exterior work in the Page model. Conceded; it is an exact contradiction.**
The rate form of `eq:page-first-law-rows`, with $A=4\pi R^2$, $E_R=R/2G$ and $2R\kappa_R=N_R\beta_R$, is $\dot E=N_R\beta_R\dot E_R+P_{\rm ext}$, as [00] prints in `sec:scaling-far`. The boundary charge is conserved by the Hartree flow (Appendix B), so $\dot E=0$, and the assumed law is $dE_R/du=-L$. Hence
$$P_{\rm ext}=N_R\beta_R\,L\,\frac{du}{dT}>0$$
at a simple outer root with $L>0$ and a forward received-time map. The vacuum family's $W_{\rm ext}=0$ compares static configurations of different $E$; the dynamical balance at conserved charge ties work to root motion, $P_{\rm ext}=0\Leftrightarrow\dot R=0$, which [00] also already states. My addition contradicted both. Conversely, embedding alone implies no work.

**1.12 The shell diagnostic 0.02 to 0.38 as the object's $\mathcal R$. Conceded.**
$\mathcal R=T_H\dot S_{\rm marg}/L$ is the no-return marginal entropy per unit energy over $T_H$ of the thermally supplied comparison. The shell number is the change of exterior-cut excess per unit shell energy flow at $16r_s$ over 40 to 100, with formation light, field work and returning content inside it, and [00] says it "classifies neither emitted entropy nor thermality". For constant $\mathcal R$ and $f$, $y_P=\mathcal R/(\mathcal R+f)$ and $S_P/S_0=f\mathcal R/(\mathcal R+f)$ are rate-independent only under one fixed entropy-per-energy relation; the time fraction uses $L=\mathcal L/R^2$. A smaller $\mathcal R$ would formally lower and delay the peak, but the diagnostic does not license the substitution, so no alternative maximum is printed.

Conclusion (i) is a held-field result, not a factorization of the evolving history; (ii) overstated: an embedded root has the definite slope $N_R\beta_R/2R$, and small $\chi$ alone supplies no vacuum exterior; (iii) stands with the checker's scope.

**A. Exterior scope (abstract clause, `sec:results-exterior` summaries, `sec:page-effective` opening).**
> Within the leading constraints, a connected inward-branch vacuum exterior with positive boundary charge and unit clock at infinity is Schwarzschild. Where that exterior contains $r_E=2GE$, its trapping radius is $r_E$ and its surface slope is $\kappa_H=1/(2r_E)$.
