### 2.2 The rows in the solver's normalization

The engine integrates U' = 8 pi g r^2 (rho_ell + q J_ell) and (ln N)' = -4 pi g r J_ell/q with q = v/N = -sqrt(U/r), and W' = 8 pi g r^2 N^2 rho_ell with N(r_c) = 1, v = -sqrt(W/r), E = W(r_c)/(2g). With M = U/(2g) = r u^2/(2g) these are M' = 4 pi r^2 (rho + u J) and N'/N = -4 pi G r J/u: the declared rows of FIRSTLAW_rows_v1 with G = g, rho = rho_ell, J = +J_ell. rho_ell and J_ell are the engine's Gaussian-kernel averages (standard deviation ell = 2) per unit volume of the stored per-site sources; eps_tot[n] is the energy of site n, not a density, and the quadrature int 4 pi r^2 rho_ell dr = sum eps_tot is checked per record (median -1.72e-08, max 6.11e-08 relative). J_ell = <P_r> = -J_r of the goal's display (the engine's dictionary line; the owner's confirmation of the amended sign of v is marked pending there). The sources are the declared averaged, reference-subtracted densities at the fixed averaging prescription; the stored applied arrays and the unsmoothed site energies cannot replace them (hreduce).

The identity is evaluated in its finite-collar form: N is normalized to 1 at the collar r_c = 2048 rather than at infinity, E is the collar charge E_c = M(r_c) = W(r_c)/(2G), and W_ext runs to r_c; with the collar fixed and N(r_c) = 1 for every record, the boundary term is delta E_c and no boundary work is missing for this fixed normalization and domain (hreduce). The tail beyond the collar enters the bookkeeping in one place only: the tail proxy |W_tail.E_tail_end - E_collar| (median 6.65e-06 over the rooted records) is one of the three terms of the per-record sigma_E and hence of the cumulative endpoint floor; the per-pair floor carries no tail term (section 2.3). Neither indicator certifies the conversion of the collar charge to an asymptotic charge: the comparison with E = M(infinity) would need the exterior continuation and the lapse normalization (N_infinity delta M_infinity = delta M_c + the work beyond r_c), and the fixed-collar identity itself needs no tail correction.

### 2.3 The three columns, the residuals, and what the estimator is

Column 1, delta E = E(T_b) - E(T_a), with E the record key E (the collar charge at r_collar = 2048). The re-solved E_collar = W(r_c)/(2g) is carried beside it; its relative difference from the stored E has median 0 and max 3.65e-09 (the flag threshold 1e-06 was never reached), and the residual with delta E_collar in place of delta E is reported separately.

Column 2, (kappa_R/8 pi G) delta A with A = 4 pi R^2, R the outer dense zero of N + v of the re-solved solution (the engine's trapping_radius, with an independent brentq scan at step ell/64 beside it), delta A = A_b - A_a. kappa_R = d_r(N + v) at R is taken from the equations the dense functions integrate (N' = -4 pi g r J_ell N^2/v, v' = (W' - W/r)/(2 r v), W' = 8 pi g r^2 N^2 rho_ell), which at the root equals the row formula (N_R/2R)[1 - 8 pi g R^2 (rho_ell(R) - J_ell(R))]; the Richardson centred difference of the dense N + v at h = ell/64 and the stored kappa_T are carried beside. kappa is evaluated at both records and averaged (the midpoint rule in the record index); the primary column uses the equations' kappa; the spread between the readings enters the floor.

Column 3, W_ext = 4 pi int from R_mid to r_c of r^2 [N_mid (rho_ell,b - rho_ell,a) + v_mid (J_ell,b - J_ell,a)] dr on the dense grid, with Eulerian source differences at fixed r, N_mid = (N_a + N_b)/2 and v_mid likewise, R_mid = (R_a + R_b)/2, r_c the re-solved collar (equal for both records, else the pair is excluded). The quadrature is the trapezoid with the partial first cell on the interpolated integrand; Simpson and the half grid are carried beside as the radial discretization reading (all three share the partial-first-cell treatment, so their agreement does not test that cell); the endpoint weights (N_a, v_a) and (N_b, v_b) beside as the record-index reading; the lower limits R_a and R_b beside (version 2 prints their sums, section 3; version 1 had them in the json only). W_ext is split into its N delta rho and v delta J parts and, from the stored eps_pairs and J_pairs smoothed with the engine's own kernel, into a pairs' part and the remainder (the md's dust part).

Residuals: res = delta E - column 2 - column 3 per pair, cumulated in order; res_noW = delta E - column 2 (the no-work premise); res_vac = delta E - delta A/(32 pi G^2 E_mid) (the vacuum first law, kappa = 1/(4GE)); res_applied is the dense-solution row-residual diagnostic: the applied identity of FIRSTLAW_rows_v1 with the row defects e = M' - 4 pi r^2 (rho + u J) and g = N'/N + 4 pi G r J/u retained through the integral of N_mid [(e_b - e_a) + g_mid (M_b - M_a)] from R_mid to r_c, the derivatives by a 4th-order stencil at spacing 0.125, evaluated on the dense fields: the exact row solutions have zero defects, an algebraic fact, and the stencil-estimated defects of the numerical dense representation are consistency readings, not bounded errors (section 6a). It is not a test of the native applied history (section 6a for the surface term a general applied extension would also need).

What the columns are. The theorem of FIRSTLAW_rows_v1 is differential; its finite form is the path integral of the identity along a smooth simple-root family of row solutions, with the moving lower limit R(lambda), kappa(lambda) and the actual intermediate fields. The averaged kappa times delta A, the midpoint weights on the Eulerian differences and the lower limit R_mid are a quadrature of that integral, a finite-record estimator, not an exact finite-increment identity. Its nominal third order per pair in the record step needs a smooth path with controlled derivatives, which was not measured; endpoint spreads and two prescriptions bracketing zero do not bound the path error (a smooth kappa with equal endpoint and midpoint samples and a nonzero integral difference is the counterexample both reviewers of version 1 give; section 10).

Floors and the proxy. Per record, sigma_E = max(stop_E |E|, |indicator.collar_screen.delta_E_signed|, |W_tail.E_tail_end - E_collar|) (medians over the rooted records 9.83e-06, 1.59e-05, 6.65e-06; sigma_E median 1.68e-05) and sigma_R = max(|R_dense - R_stored|, |R_scan - R_engine|, |N + v|(R)/kappa) (median 2.6e-14 sites). Per pair, sigma_dE = sqrt(stop_E^2 (E_a^2 + E_b^2) + (screen_b - screen_a)^2), which carries the stopping tolerance and the screen difference only and no tail term; sigma_col2 = (kappa_mean R_mid/G) sqrt(sigma_R_a^2 + sigma_R_b^2) in quadrature with |delta A|/(8 pi G) times the largest kappa spread; floor = sqrt(sigma_dE^2 + sigma_col2^2); pull = res/floor. The reported record-step discretization proxy: disc_kappa = |column 2 with kappa_b - column 2 with kappa_a|/2, disc_W_rule = max(|W_trapz - W_simpson|, |W_trapz - W_half|), disc_W_lambda = |W(N_b, v_b) - W(N_a, v_a)|/2, disc their quadrature sum, pull_total = res/sqrt(floor^2 + disc^2). The proxy does not contain the lower-limit variants R_a, R_b or the midpoint-field kappa, which are computed beside it. The cumulative floor is sqrt(sigma_E_first^2 + sigma_E_last^2 + sum sigma_col2^2); the cumulative disc is the plain sum. Neither is a proved bound, a statistical confidence level or a measured convergence; the root-sum-square combinations have no proved independence or covariance (hreduce).

The pairwise differences of the tail proxies, read beside the pair floor: W_tail_dE has |b - a| median 4.16e-06 and max 0.00151 over the 224 pairs and exceeds the pair floor in 5 of them; sigma_E_tail has |b - a| median 3.48e-06, max 0.00151, and exceeds the pair floor in 3 of them. These are the sizes a pairwise tail term would have; the pair floor as defined contains none, and the fixed-collar identity needs none.

### 2.7 The version 3 columns as computed

T_ll. In the orthonormal normal and radial frame T_nn = rho, T_ns = -J, T_ss = P_r and ell = n + s, so T_ll = rho + P_r - 2J, with no positivity premise for these averaged, reference-subtracted sources (hreduce, both documents of both rounds). With the rows' J = +J_ell this is T_ll = rho_ell + p_ell - 2 J_ell, evaluated at the outer dense root R of each rooted record (and at its inner zero), with rho_ell and J_ell the exact kernel values the columns already read there and p_ell the radial stress at R. The stress is the key S_r_ell of the companion npz collapse_<tag>_handoff.npz beside the fields npz: the engine's own kernel average per unit volume of the radial stress on the companion's node grid r_nodes (1025 nodes at spacing 2, range [0, 2048]), its rows matched to the records by T (226 of 226 matched, 0 unmatched, at T_match_tol 1e-06), read as a density at scale 1, carried to the dense grid and to the zeros by a not-a-knot cubic spline through the nodes (end values held outside the node range; the md's count of dense cells outside the node range has median 0; the range covers the dense grid). No kernel is applied here: rho_ell and J_ell at the root are exact kernel values and p_r(R) is a spline value, so T_ll(R) is a reconstructed value carrying the stress's between-node interpolation remainder, for which the run gives an interpolation proxy per record: the stride-2 test (the spline through every other node against the stored values at the skipped nodes, divided by 2^4 for the full node density, that is, an extrapolation of the coarse-node difference under fourth-order scaling and not a remainder bound; over the grid and within the window around R), checked by the local polynomial of degree 4 at R and by T_ll at the two nodes bracketing R, where the stress is the stored node value and only rho_ell and J_ell are interpolated from the dense grid at spacing 0.125; all three are functions of the same node samples (section 6b). Beside T_ll: rho_ell + p_ell + 2 J_ell (the component along the other null direction n - s), rho_ell - 2 J_ell alone (the part without the stress), 8 pi G R^2 T_ll (the dimensionless form; the row formula's margin is 1 - 8 pi G R^2 (rho - J)), the profile across the root at R + (-2, -1, -0.5, 0, 0.5, 1, 2) ell and on the window |r - R| <= 4 ell at the dense spacing (its minimum, maximum, sign changes and the sign change nearest R); per pair the values at both roots, their mean (the midpoint rule in the record index) and the midpoint fields' value at R_mid. The tangential stress S_t_ell is read at R and reported only; T_ll does not use it. The flux: F = 4 pi r^2 [(N^2 + v^2) J + N v (rho + p_r)] of the continuity identity, at the root -4 pi R^2 N_R^2 T_ll (v = -N), the Eulerian radial flux at the instantaneous root, at the collar from the dense arrays.

The version-3 run had computed rho_ell - 2 J_ell in place of T_ll, the fields npz carrying no stress; version 3b reads the handoff instead, and its rho - 2J median at the outer root (-1.73e-05) reappears exactly.

The row defect at every dense root. e = M' - 4 pi r^2 (rho + u J) (the energy row; M = r u^2/(2G), u = v/N) and g = N'/N + 4 pi G r J/u (the momentum row), read from the arrays e, g on the dense grid (the 4th-order stencil at spacing 0.125 that the applied identity's defect integral uses) by the local polynomial of degree 4 at every dense zero of N + v, inner and outer; the relative forms divide by the rows' own terms there, 4 pi z^2 (|rho| + |u J|) and |N'/N| + 4 pi G z |J/u|. Beside e(R): the same defect read from the two kappas already in the columns, e_root_from_kappa = -(kappa_fd - kappa_formula) R/(N_R G), which follows from (N + v)' = N(1 - 2GM')/(2R) at u = -1 with M' the Richardson derivative at h = ell/64, an alternative estimate of e on the same root, fields and units (hreduce); the applied identity's correction of kappa, -N_R G e(R)/R (the -2Ge term of kappa-tilde), carried into column 2 as (delta A/(8 pi G)) times its pair mean.
