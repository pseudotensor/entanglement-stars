## Caption draft (recommended, four sentences, no semicolons)

Cut through the computed entanglement star from the regular centre outward, with radii linear to \(r_s\) and logarithmic beyond, and with the interior taken from the \(T/r_s=15\) slice of Fig.~\ref{fig:star-collapse}(a). The trapping surface \(R_t\) formed inside the matter at \(T/r_s\approx12\) and sits at 0.95 of \(2GE\), and inside it the hydrodynamic reading of the fields has ended, so only the lattice equations hold there: the dust (640 fermions, central density at most 3.2 percent of the exclusion density) fills the regular centre and the grey trapped region, where outgoing long waves move inward, while lattice modes of short wavelength cross the surface outward with a delay logarithmic in the spacing, and the entanglement across the cut supplies \(A/4G\) at a spacing near two Planck lengths. Outside, the derived vacuum exterior is Schwarzschild's with the prepared Dirac sea as its vacuum, the dust was released at \(4r_s\), where a static receiver reads the redshift, and the massless light field develops outgoing flow read at the \(16r_s\) cut. The exponential ray-map segment carrying \(T_H\) and the centrifugal barrier near \(3GE\) are results on that exterior, not resolved on the computed collapse, and the averaging width \(\sigma=4a\) is drawn to scale against \(r_s=64a\).
