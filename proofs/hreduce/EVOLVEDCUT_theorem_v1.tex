\noindent\textbf{An evolved cut: an exact excitation bound and a conditional area estimate.}
Use the full native one-particle space, a complementary cut $X,X^c$, and
the same mass $\mu=ma>0$, parent, applied adjoints and occupation convention.
Let $C$ be the physical pure number-conserving Gaussian covariance and $P$ the compatible
continued spectral projector. In nats, whenever the traces exist,
\[
 |S_X(C)-S_X(P)|\leq 2\|\Pi_X(C-P)\Pi_{X^c}\|_1
 \leq\nu,\qquad \nu=\|C-P\|_1.
\]
A rank-$r$ covariance change, or addition/removal of $r$ cut modes, costs
at most $r\log2$. A particle--hole rotation, or a unitary on one mode
on each side, costs at most $2\log2$. Expected pair number alone gives
no such linear bound.
For mixed number-conserving Gaussian covariances on $d_X$ modes, with $h_2$
the binary entropy, the valid bound is
$d_X h_2(\min\{\|C_X-P_X\|_1/d_X,1/2\})$ instead.

For $h=\{n,h_0\}/2+\sum_i\{w_i,Q_i\}/2$, the constant-coefficient
band separation is $2n\sqrt{m^2+a^{-2}\sum_i\sin^2k_i}\geq2n_*m$,
including $|w|=n$. This does not establish the global spectral-gap
premise of the paired massive local-area theorem. Under that theorem's
actual pathwise global gap, $n\geq n_*>0$, bounded fields, first and
second derivatives of orders $L^{-1},L^{-2}$, infinite-parent and regular-cut
premises, write $A_{\rm lat}=4\pi(R/a)^2$ and
$e_{\rm flat}=|S_X(P_0)/A_{\rm lat}-\eta_{\rm sph}(\mu)|$. Then
\[
 \left|\frac{S_X(C)}{A_{\rm lat}}-\eta_{\rm sph}(\mu)\right|
 \leq e_{\rm flat}
 +C_{\rm loc}\left(\frac aL\right)^2
       \left(1+\left|\log\frac aL\right|\right)^4
 +\frac{\min\{\nu,r\log2,d_X\log2\}}{A_{\rm lat}}.
\]
Here $r=\operatorname{rank}(C_X-P_X)$ when finite. At $r_s/a=64$,
$L=\lambda_Lr_s$, $R=\lambda_Rr_s$, the last two prefactors are exactly
$(1+|\log(64\lambda_L)|)^4/(64\lambda_L)^2$ and
$1/(16384\pi\lambda_R^2)$. The theorem supplies no numerical
$C_{\rm loc}$ or actual-history $\nu$. Neither the local band gap nor
positive lapse alone supplies them. For $L=R=r_s$, the local term is
about $0.173C_{\rm loc}$. At the paired nominal solar conversion ($G/a^2=1/4$),
$a/r_s=1.09456\,10^{-38}$, the same prefactors are about
$7.3\,10^{-69}$ and $9.5\,10^{-78}$, only for a family with uniform
hypotheses and controlled excitation content, not a history rescaling.
The reference may not be replaced by an instantaneous Killing vacuum
without its separate cut/projector comparison. No small evolved-area
error at the run's parameters has thereby been established.
