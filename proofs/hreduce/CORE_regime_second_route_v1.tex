\noindent\textbf{A homogeneous core comparison and its native limitation.}
Let $\mu=ma$, $g=G/a^2=1/4$, $N=1$ and the radial momentum source vanish.
Fill both positive normal-energy spin states in each of the eight native
momentum balls $|q|=|a\delta k|\leq\pi/4$. For a homogeneous positive source,
regular centre and normalized source averaging, the bulk phase count and
spherical Gauss row give
\[
 n=\frac{\pi}{24a^3},\quad
 \frac{R_F^3}{a^3}=\frac{18N_B}{\pi^2},\quad
 v=-Hr,\quad H^2=\frac{8\pi G\rho}{3}.
\]
$R_F$ is the volume radius $N_B=n(4\pi R_F^3/3)$, not a centroid or
trapping radius. The additional dust closure $\rho=mn$ gives
$\sigma|v'|=(\pi/6)(\sigma/a)\sqrt\mu$, independent of $N_B$.
At an ideal sharp vacuum match $v'$ changes from $-H$ to $H/2$;
this does not halve the full spherical strain norm.
For the same filling with its actual native normal energy,
\[
 \bar e=\left\langle\sqrt{\mu^2+\sum_i\sin^2q_i}\right\rangle,
 \qquad \bar e\geq\sqrt{\mu^2+9/32},\qquad
 \sigma|v'|=\frac\pi6\frac\sigma a\sqrt{\bar e}
                 >0.38\frac\sigma a .
\]
Thus this positive homogeneous comparison fails the $0.1$ gradient test
for $\sigma/a\geq2$, even as $\mu\to0$. It is not an obstruction theorem
for an actual core with nonuniform occupations, currents, lapse and a
continued-reference contribution. In a uniform applied field the same
spherical filling has $\langle\dot x_i\rangle=24v_i/\pi^3$, directed
with the inward flow; the vanishing axial flow derivative at $q_i=\pi/4$
does not establish a bulk rebound.

For the supplied continuum $d=16$ comparator
$E_{\max}=c_E/(G^{3/2}m^2)$, $c_E=0.135858$, and the actual charge
radius $r_s=2GE$, at $m>0$,
\[
 E>E_{\max}\iff\frac{r_s}{a}>\frac{2c_E}{\sqrt g\,\mu^2}.
\]
At $\sigma/a=2$, the dust test gives $\mu\leq9/(100\pi^2)$; its overlap
with that comparator exists only for
$r_s/a>40000c_E\pi^4/81\simeq6.5\,10^3$.
This conditional dust window at order $10^4$ is neither native
hydrodynamic control nor a collapse or evolved-core conclusion.
