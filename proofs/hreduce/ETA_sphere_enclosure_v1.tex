\noindent\textbf{Spherical coefficient and physical spacing.}
For the flat massless occupied sine--Dirac sea on the infinite cubic
parent, use nats, one four-component field and one planar face; the full
Brillouin zone already includes all eight valleys. The native digital-ball
theorem gives, uniformly in the centre offset,
\[
 S(A_R)=\eta_{\rm sph}{4\pi R^2\over a^2}+o(R^2/a^2),\qquad
 \eta_{\rm sph}={1\over4\pi}\int_{S^2}\eta(n)\,d\Omega .
\]
Strong subadditivity and the boundary-cancelled Lov\'asz
limit make $|v|\eta(v/|v|)=h_K(v)$ a symmetric convex support function.
For valid intervals $l_i\leq\eta(n_i)\leq u_i$ at unit directions
including both signs of the axes, set
$P=\{x:n_i\cdot x\leq u_i\}$ and
$C_i=P\cap\{x:n_i\cdot x\geq l_i\}$. Then
\[
 \max_i\inf_{x\in C_i}n\cdot x\leq\eta(n)\leq h_P(n),\qquad
 {1\over4\pi}\int h_P\,d\Omega={1\over8\pi}\sum_e |e|\theta_e,
\]
where $\theta_e$ is the exterior normal angle. The executed upper bound
uses outward affine integration majorizing this polyhedral mean; it was
not an edge-sum computation.
