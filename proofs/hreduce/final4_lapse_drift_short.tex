\subsection*{Corrected lapse and boundary-energy drift}
Choose a finite unit collar, fixed-site quantum state $\rho$, complete
interior clock rows $c$, and the same-law boundary functional
$\mathcal B$.  Its positive-source extension
$\mathcal Q=\sum_xe_x/\psi_x$ obeys
$\mathcal B=\mathcal Q-\psi^{-1}\cdot c$ off constraint.
On $c=0$, with $J=\partial_\psi c|_\rho$ invertible, the ruler row
of $\mathcal H=\mathcal B+N\cdot c$ gives
\[
 N_*=-J^{-T}\mathcal B_\psi
     =\psi^{-1}-J^{-T}\mathcal Q_\psi .
\]
Derivatives retain the collar and all reference/filter responses;
on-constraint equality of $\mathcal B$ and $\mathcal Q$ does not
permit replacing their off-constraint derivatives.

Choose matter evolution generated by $\mathcal H$ using
$\{F,G\}=(i\hbar)^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}\rho[F_\rho,G_\rho]$,
at fixed collar controls.  Put
$b=\{c,\mathcal B\}$, $\Omega=\{c,c\}=-\Omega^T$ and
$v_*=b+\Omega N_*$.  Clock preservation gives
$J\dot\psi=-b-\Omega N$.  The direct bracket chain rule gives
\[
 \dot{\mathcal B}
 =\mathcal B_\psi^T\dot\psi-b^TN
 =(N-N_*)^TJ\dot\psi
 =-(N-N_*)^Tv_* .
\]
Independently, $\dot{\mathcal H}
=c\cdot\dot N+(\mathcal B_\psi+J^TN)\cdot\dot\psi$
gives the same identity on $c=0$.
[X: state-bracket chain rule; total-energy chain rule.]
Thus $N=N_*$ conserves the charge; conservation alone also allows
$N=N_*+Av_*$ with $A^T=-A$.
Preservation and conservation do not in general select the lapse uniquely.
Positive lapse and regular classical-ruler evolution remain domain
conditions; no operator-ruler time law is inferred.
