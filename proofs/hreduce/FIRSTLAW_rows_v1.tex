\noindent\textbf{First-law variation from the declared spherical rows.}
Set $u=v/N<0$, $M=ru^2/(2G)$, $N_\infty=1$, $E=M_\infty$;
$\rho,J$ are the averaged, normal-ordered sources. At fixed $G$ and
averaging prescription, the declared rows
$M'=4\pi r^2(\rho+uJ)$, $N'/N=-4\pi GrJ/u$ and
$\delta u=G\delta M/(ru)$ give, for smooth Eulerian variations with $N,M>0$,
\[
 (N\delta M)'=4\pi r^2(N\delta\rho+v\delta J).
\]
At a differentiable simple dense root $u(R)=-1$,
$\delta M_R=(1/(2G)-M'_R)\delta R$. With $A=4\pi R^2$ and convergent integrals,
\begin{gather*}
 \delta E=\frac{\kappa_R}{8\pi G}\delta A+W_{\rm ext},\qquad
 W_{\rm ext}=4\pi\int_R^\infty r^2(N\delta\rho+v\delta J)\,dr,\\
 \kappa_R=\frac{N_R}{2R}\{1-8\pi GR^2(\rho_R-J_R)\}
          =\left.\partial_r(N+v)\right|_R=\kappa_0.
\end{gather*}
This is the same-field dense collar slope $\kappa_T$, not a temperature
claim. A double root gives $8\pi G R^2(\rho_R-J_R)=1$; a fold also needs
radial second-derivative and parameter-transversality nondegeneracy.

For applied fields (tildes), define
$e=\widetilde M'-4\pi r^2(\rho+\widetilde uJ)$ and
$g=\widetilde N'/\widetilde N+4\pi GrJ/\widetilde u$.
The applied simple-root identity retains both row defects:
\begin{align*}
 \delta\widetilde E
 &=\frac{\widetilde\kappa}{8\pi G}\delta\widetilde A
   +\widetilde W_{\rm ext}
   +\int_{\widetilde R}^{\infty}\widetilde N
                    (\delta e+g\delta\widetilde M)\,dr,\\
 \widetilde\kappa
 &=\left.\frac{\widetilde N}{2r}
       \{1-8\pi Gr^2(\rho-J)-2Ge\}\right|_{\widetilde R}
   =\left.\partial_r(\widetilde N+\widetilde v)\right|_{\widetilde R}.
\end{align*}
$\widetilde E=E$ requires preserved asymptotic charge and normalization.
For time tangents, converting $W_{\rm ext}/dt$ to outward energy fluxes
$\mathcal F(R)-\mathcal F(\infty)$ also retains
$4\pi\int_R^\infty r^2(NW_0+vW_r)\,dr$, with the same-field energy
and momentum continuity residuals. Neither pair of defects is presumed zero.
