\subsection*{A sufficient clock-preserving time law}
Choose commuting ruler profiles $\psi$, fixed-site quantum matter
coordinates $z$ with their ordinary symplectic 1-form $\vartheta$,
and the same-law off-constraint generator
$H=B(\psi,z)+N^TC(\psi,z)$.
Let $J=C_\psi$ be invertible on the clock surface, with fixed collar
and controls.
The additional principle that the rulers are auxiliary, with no
independent kinetic or Berry term, defines the action
\[
 I=\int[\vartheta(z)\dot z-B(\psi,z)-N^TC(\psi,z)]\,dT .
\]
Its ruler equation derives $N_*=-J^{-T}B_\psi$.
With $C=0$ solved as $\psi=\phi(z)$, implicit differentiation gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_zB_{\rm red}&=B_z-C_z^TJ^{-T}B_\psi
               =B_z+C_z^TN_*,\\
 B_{\rm red}&=B(\phi(z),z).
 \end{aligned}
\]
Thus ordinary Hamiltonian evolution by $B_{\rm red}$, together with
$\dot\psi=-J^{-1}C_z\dot z$, preserves the clock row.
Autonomous reduced evolution conserves $B_{\rm red}$ and preserves
the quantum-state norm on its domain.
This is a local commuting-profile/quantum-matter reduction, not a
linear quantum theory of noncommuting rulers.
[X: auxiliary-ruler variational reduction; implicit reduced-energy
differentiation.]

Preservation and conservation alone do not generally select this law.
For the canonical matter bracket define
$b=\{C,B\}$, $\Omega=\{C,C\}=-\Omega^T$ and
$v_*=b+\Omega N_*$.
The constraint chain rule and the boundary-energy derivative give
\[
 J\dot\psi=-b-\Omega N,\qquad
 \dot B=-(N-N_*)^Tv_* .
\]
Where the dimension permits, every $N=N_*+Av_*$ with
$A^T=-A$ also has zero drift.
Positivity of lapse must still be checked.
[X: matter-bracket chain rule; total-energy chain rule.]

A concrete action counterfamily keeps the same static rows but
changes the symplectic structure.
Take canonical oscillator coordinates $(q,p)$ and 2 rulers with
$C=(\psi_1-\psi_{01}-q,\psi_2-\psi_{02}-p)^T$.
Choose positive constants $\psi_{0i},N_{0i}$ and
$B=(q^2+p^2)/2-N_0^TC$.
Add the ruler 1-form $\lambda(\psi_1-\psi_{01})\dot\psi_2$ to
the action above, with $0\le\lambda<1$.
All static equations are unchanged.
On $C=0$ its kinetic term is
$p\dot q+\lambda q\dot p$, equivalent up to a total derivative to
$(1-\lambda)p\dot q$.
The oscillator frequency is therefore $(1-\lambda)^{-1}$, while
its positive reduced energy remains $(q^2+p^2)/2$.
The lapse vector is $N=N_0-\lambda(q,p)^T/(1-\lambda)$, positive
on a sufficiently small orbit; both rulers remain positive there.
This is a logical countermodel to static selection, not an
alternative microscopic $H_*$.

For a strong quantum constraint represented by a moving physical
projector $P(T)$, preservation instead requires
$\dot P=[K,P]/(i\hbar)$.
Its off-diagonal solution is
$K=i\hbar[\dot P,P]+K_{\rm diag}$ with
$[K_{\rm diag},P]=0$.
An instantaneous eigenprojector commuting with $K$ therefore cannot
follow a moving prescribed projector without a connection term.
For a fixed physical projector with weak constraints $C_xP=0$,
central lapse terms cannot
repair leakage:
$(1-P)(B+\sum_xN_xC_x)P=(1-P)BP$.
H2 does not supply the missing projector transport or the
block-diagonal generator.
