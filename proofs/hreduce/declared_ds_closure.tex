\paragraph{Energy algebra [X; radial counterexample R].}
Let $h=\beta m+\alpha_i\sin(ap_i)/a$, $E_x=\tfrac12\{P_x,h\}$,
$H[f]=c^\dagger h[f]c$, and $h[f]=\tfrac12\{f,h\}$.
For every pair of sites, CAR gives the exact identity
\[
 [\mathcal H_x,\mathcal H_y]
 =\tfrac14 c^\dagger\bigl(P_xhP_yh+P_xh^2P_y+hP_xhP_y
                         -(x\leftrightarrow y)\bigr)c .
\]
Its full Fourier form is particularly short.  For smearings $e^{iqx},e^{ikx}$,
write $p$ for the midpoint of the input and output momenta, put
$Q_i(p)=\sin(2ap_i)/(2a)$, $c_i=\cos(ap_i)$, and define
\[
 b_i={c_i\over a}\cos(aq_i/2)\sin(ak_i/2),\qquad
 d_i={c_i\over a}\cos(ak_i/2)\sin(aq_i/2).
\]
With $[\alpha_i,\alpha_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}\Sigma_k$, the commutator vertex is
\[
 [h[e^{iqx}],h[e^{ikx}]](p)
 ={2\over a}\sum_i\cos(aq_i/2)\cos(ak_i/2)
       \sin\!\bigl(a(k_i-q_i)/2\bigr)Q_i(p)
   +2i\boldsymbol\Sigma\cdot(\boldsymbol d\mathbin\times\boldsymbol b).
 \tag{1}
\]
All mass terms cancel.  In particular, the axis-longitudinal quadrature pair
$f=\cos(qx_i)$, $g=\sin(qx_i)$ obeys, at every lattice momentum and mass,
\[
 i[H[f],H[g]]={\sin(2aq)+2\sin(aq)\over4a}\,c^\dagger Q_i c
 =q\left[1-{5\over12}(aq)^2+O((aq)^4)\right]c^\dagger Q_i c . \tag{2}
\]
Thus the current is the two-step identity-channel current, with unit valley
slope.  It is not the one-step number-current vertex.

For smooth general smearings, set $v=f\nabla g-g\nabla f$ and
$C_i=\cos(ap_i)$.  Equation (1) gives
\[
 i[H[f],H[g]]=c^\dagger\left[
 {1\over2}\{v_i,Q_i\}
 +{1\over8}\sum_{ijk}\epsilon_{kij}
     \{\partial_i v_j,\Sigma_k C_iC_j\}\right]c+\mathcal R . \tag{3}
\]
For shape momenta of size $q$, the orbital remainder is
$O(a^2q^3|Q|)$ and the spin remainder $O(a^2q^4)$.
The spin term is $\tfrac14(\nabla\times v)\cdot\boldsymbol\Sigma$ with
the displayed valley dressing; omitting it leaves a relative $O(qa)$
full-zone error, whereas retaining it leaves $O((qa)^2)$ for generic shapes.
An irrotational continuum flow has no spin term at this order.
Exact closure on $Q_i$ alone fails even for radial smearings. Take
$f=\chi(r/L)r^2/L^2$, $g=\chi(r/L)r^6/L^6$, with a smooth radial cutoff
$\chi=1$ for $r\leq3L$ and $\chi=0$ for $r\geq4L$.
For $L/a$ an integer at least 4, $x=(L,2L,0)$ and
$y=x+a(e_1+e_2)$, the full spinor kernel gives exactly
\[
 \bigl(i[h[f],h[g]]\bigr)_{xy}
 =-{a^2(2L+a)(3L+a)(4L+a)\over4L^7}\,\Sigma_3\ne0. \tag{4}
\]
Every scalar two-step $Q_i$ current has zero block at this corner link.
The spherical continuum dictionary survives; its finite-spacing radial
algebra has the explicit spin and allocation remainders given below.
