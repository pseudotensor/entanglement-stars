\subsection*{Uniform-flow vacuum identity}
Choose the identical-Dirac-block insertion
$h_v=h_0+\operatorname{diag}(s[v],s[v])$,
$s[v]=\tfrac12\{v^i,Q_i\}$, $Q_i(p)=\sin(2ap_i)/(2a)$,
on one full periodic sine lattice per positive-mass packet.
Let $H_*[v]$ add its canonical shift lift to $H_*$.
For uniform positive $N,\psi$ and constant $v$, the scalar
$s(p)=v\cdot Q(p)$ commutes with each band projector.
The filled sea therefore remains an eigenstate, and paired momenta give
\[
 \delta W=\operatorname{Tr}
   [P_-\operatorname{diag}(s,s)]
   =\operatorname{Tr}s=0,\qquad
 H_*[v]|\Omega\rangle=W_0|\Omega\rangle .
\]
The trace identity also follows from the two diagonal blocks
$P_{-,11}=P_{-,22}=I/2$; empty number-conserving bosons contribute zero.
[X: paired band-spectrum sum; polar-projector trace.]
Ground-energy equality additionally requires that this state remain
the ground state; unchanged free-band occupation does not establish
that for an interacting spectrum.

For arbitrary positive prescribed rulers and $v(x)$ the reference
expectation still obeys $W_{\rm ref}=W_0$, without guaranteeing
an invariant sea.  About a homogeneous gapped reference with
oriented polar factor $U$, the interband vertex is
\[
 \mathcal V_{+-}(p+q,p)
 =\tfrac12s_{p+q,p}
       [I-U^\dagger(p+q)U(p)]=O(q).
\]
Thus a local analytic \emph{quadratic relaxed-ground response} has
$K_{vv}(q)=O(q^2)$ by its spectral sum of squared vertices over
positive excitation gaps.  This is the justified gradient onset.
The uniform identity alone fixes no nonzero gradient coefficient,
no all-amplitude flow functional, and no conserved interacting
lattice momentum.
