\subsection*{What selects the allocation exponent}
Choose the power family
$A_z=m\psi^{-z}+i\psi^{-(z+2)}D$,
$L_z=N\psi^z$, positive prescribed rulers and clocks, and the
common grouped mass rectangle.
$D$ is one full cubic sine operator, with its 8 continuum valleys
counted once; the positive squared masses are
$(x_0,x_0+h_1,x_0+h_2,x_0+h_1+h_2)$ with weights $(+,-,-,+)$.
Write $X_z=\sum_s\eta_s(|A_{s,z}^\dagger|-|A_{s,z}|)$.
Every $z$ has the same uniform excitation readout
$N\sqrt{m^2+\psi^{-4}d(p)^2}$ and the exact isospectral identity
$W_z=0$ at constant $L_z$.
Neither property selects $z$.
At a flat stationary reference, twice differentiating this null curve
gives the finite-matrix identity
\[
 K_{\psi\psi}=z(K_{N\psi}+K_{\psi N}),\qquad K_{NN}=0.
\]
Direct expansion of $\operatorname{Tr}[(N\psi^z)X_z[\psi]]$, with
$X_z[1]=0$ and $\operatorname{Tr}X_z=0$, gives the same identity.

In the smooth, pole-free continuum domain at 2 spatial derivatives,
compensated coordinate covariance makes the local bulk form
$-\int[aN|\nabla\psi|^2+b\psi\nabla N\cdot\nabla\psi]$;
the constant-$L_z$ identity imposes $a=zb$.
The flat Sylvester response fixes
$b=\kappa_z=(z+2)J/\pi^2$, where
$J=\int_0^{h_1}\int_0^{h_2}(x_0+u+v)^{-1}du\,dv>0$.
Consequently
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_z^{[2]}&=-\kappa_z\!\int
 [zN|\nabla\psi|^2+\psi\nabla N\cdot\nabla\psi],\\
 (W_z^{[2]})_N&=\kappa_z
 [\psi\Delta\psi+(1-z)|\nabla\psi|^2].
 \end{aligned}
\]
An independent density reconstruction writes the grouped spin trace
$x_z^{[2]}=u_z(\psi)\Delta\psi+v_z(\psi)|\nabla\psi|^2$.
The Sylvester response at every uniform $P$, with $p=P^2k$,
gives $u_z(P)=\kappa_zP^{1-z}$; the exact integrated trace
identity on arbitrary slow periodic profiles gives $v_z=u_z'$.
Then $(W_z^{[2]})_N=\psi^zx_z^{[2]}$ directly, without varying
the reconstructed functional.
[X: covariant functional/null identity; arbitrary-$P$ Sylvester
density/trace identity.]
Requiring every positive nonconstant harmonic ruler on a source-free
patch to satisfy this clock row selects $z=1$, provided
$\kappa_z\ne0$.
For example,
$\psi_\epsilon=(1-2\epsilon c\cdot x+
\epsilon^2|c|^2|x|^2)^{-1/2}$
is harmonic away from its pole; its clock residual at $x=0$ is
$\kappa_z(1-z)\epsilon^2|c|^2$.
The degenerate $z=-2$ has zero stiffness and cannot meet a nonzero
Newton normalization.
The necessary selecting input is this harmonic-vacuum condition;
it is not supplied by quantum mechanics or positivity.
At finite spacing, even the selected $z=1$ has its coefficient-running
correction, so the exact local harmonic statement has only the stated
continuum scope.

The first-order elastic light response supplies no independent

\]
It vanishes on the elastic shell; $(z,\theta)=(1,0)$ and
$(2,1/4)$ are explicit indistinguishable examples there.
At fixed $\theta$, an informative elastic channel can constrain $z$
through $\alpha$; off-shell responses retain the displayed term.
