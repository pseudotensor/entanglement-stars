\subsection*{Dressed-excitation count}
Fix a classical ruler $0<p\leq\psi\leq P$, minimum packet mass $m>0$,
and $M$ canonical modes.  The factorization
$A=\psi^{-2}(m+iK)\psi$, $K=\psi^{-1}D\psi^{-1}=K^\dagger$,
gives both polar moduli the lapse-independent gap $\Delta_0=mp/P^2$.
For the number-preserving Hermitian similarity $T=e^{S_*}>0$,
$F_i=T^{-1}d_iT$ need not be canonical.
The boundary charge is $Q=F^\dagger\mathcal E F$ with
$\mathcal E\geq\Delta_0I$.  Sectorwise,
$F_iv=T_{n-1}^{-1}d_iT_nv$ and
$\sum_i\|d_iT_nv\|^2=n\|T_nv\|^2$, hence
\[
 Q\geq\Delta_*n,\qquad
 \Delta_*=\Delta_0\min\!\left(1,\inf_{n\geq1}
 \frac{\sigma_{\min}(T_n)^2}{\|T_{n-1}\|^2}\right)>0 .
\]
Uniform $\Delta_*>0$ in region size is an additional premise,
not a consequence of the polar gap.
For an invariant constraint restriction in this Fock space, put
$B=(E+\delta E)/\Delta_*$.
Bosonic occupation counting and the independent thermal trace bound
$\Omega\leq e^{\beta(E+\delta E)}\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta Q}$
give respectively
\[
 \Omega\leq {M+\lfloor B\rfloor\choose\lfloor B\rfloor},
 \qquad \Omega\leq z^{-B}(1-z)^{-M}\quad(0<z<1).
\]
Here $z=e^{-\beta\Delta_*}$.  Choosing $z=B/(M+B)$ yields
$\log\Omega=O(E\log E)$ on nonempty windows with $\delta E=O(E)$
and polynomial $M(E)$ at fixed microscopic parameters.

This counts positive-norm dressed excitations, not graded sea-cut
entropy or additional quantum-ruler registers.
