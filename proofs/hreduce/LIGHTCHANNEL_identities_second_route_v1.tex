\noindent\textbf{Light-field extension: identities and necessary limits.}
Use $\hbar=c=1$. For two independent CAR fields, even bilinears commute across fields.
Thus their fixed-background energy brackets add, including each native
spin/allocation remainder and reference central term; no full coupled
deformation algebra follows. With the same geometric fields and each
field's complete source adjoint and continued spectral reference,
\[
 q=\sum_{j=1}^2 A_j(C_j-P_j),\quad h_j=A_j^*(N,v),\qquad
 \delta E=\sum_j\operatorname{Tr}(h_j\delta C_j),\quad
 i\dot C_j=[h_j,C_j].
\]
This holds on the regular positive-mass, differentiable spectral/trace
domain. The source rows and their continuity defects add at fixed geometry;
the solved geometry and boundary energy are nonlinear functions of the sum.
An initially product Gaussian Hartree state remains a product, and
$S_X=S_{1,X}+S_{2,X}$. For the same cut and coefficient domains,
\[
 a_0^2=4G\eta(ma_0):\quad
 \frac{S_{2,X}^{\rm lead}}{A/(4G)}=\frac{\eta(0)}{\eta(ma_0)};

is the \emph{different}, both-fields-as-matter match. Only in the equal
massless-coefficient limit does it give $a=2\sqrt{2\eta(0)}\,\ell_P
\simeq2.71\ell_P$. The owner retains $a_0$ for the printed one-field match.

it need not coincide with the geometric root at finite averaging scale.

\[
