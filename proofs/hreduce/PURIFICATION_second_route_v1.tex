\noindent\textbf{Held evolution: local entropy relaxation and a conditional window.}
Hold the complete regular self-adjoint native $h$ fixed, including its
interior and actual source-adjoint fields. For a pure number-conserving
Gaussian covariance $C_0$, let $U=e^{-iht}$, $C_t=UC_0U^*$, and let
$P_g$ be its commuting Killing-ground projector,
with a fixed pure filling of zero modes. This diagnostic is distinct from
the continued source reference. For the complete complementary cut
$X=Y^c$, $Q=\Pi_Y$ of finite rank $d$, write
$s(x)=-x\ln x-(1-x)\ln(1-x)$ and
$F_d(z)=d\,s(\min\{z/d,1/2\})$. Then, in nats,
\[
 |S_X(C_t)-S_X(P_g)|
 \leq F_d(\|Q(C_t-P_g)Q\|_1)
 \leq F_d\!\left(\operatorname{Tr}QU|C_0-P_g|U^*Q\right).
\]
On an infinite parent, compact $C_0-P_g$ supported in the absolutely
continuous sector makes the right side tend to zero, with no rate.
Bound content, its coherences and singular-continuous content remain
separate. A finite closed parent supplies no such asymptotic conclusion.

Suppose a pure Gaussian comparison $\widetilde C_t$ is obtained from
$P_g$ by separate side unitaries and a remaining unitary acting on
$r_X,r_Y$ modes across the cut. If
$\|Q(C_t-\widetilde C_t)Q\|_1\leq\epsilon$, then
\[
 |S_X(C_t)-S_X(P_g)|
 \leq2\min(r_X,r_Y)\ln2+F_d(\epsilon).
\]
Only an actual common-history completion bound $\tau_*$ restricts these
unfinished modes to first crossings in $(t-\tau_*,t]$. Independent,
isolated Schmidt pairs with fixed weights give the sharper sum
$\sum_{j\,\mathrm{unfinished}}s(\nu_j)$, with the stated background and
residual terms. Entropy is not signed energy or expected particle count.
Neither the one-ended stationary-vacuum theorem nor the conditional
annulus estimate $2r_s\ln(1/(a\omega))+O(r_s)$ supplies $\tau_*$.
These results give no
area-capacity law or finite deadline for an evolving collapse.
