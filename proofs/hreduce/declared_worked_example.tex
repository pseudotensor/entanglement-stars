\paragraph{Point-source example [X; averaging R].}
Take a stationary vacuum region of the declared continuum flat-slice
constraints, outside a localized source and its averaging support.
The angular-averaged sources use the continued occupied spectral reference,
on a sheet where it commutes with the matter Hamiltonian.
For a fixed bare source array on the regular $J=0$, $N=1$ branch,
normalized compact averaging preserves the boundary energy
$E=\int\rho\,d^3x$. At fixed $E$, this vacuum geometry is independent of $\ell$.
For $v=v(r)\boldsymbol n$, the principal curvatures
are $v'/N,v/(Nr),v/(Nr)$.  The two vacuum rows reduce to
\[
 0={vN'\over4\pi G rN^2},\qquad
 0={1\over8\pi G r^2}{d\over dr}(rv^2),
\]
after the asymptotic clock fixes $N=1$.  The inward branch and the source
boundary charge therefore give $v=-\sqrt{r_s/r}$, $r_s=2GE$.
The declared continuum metric reads
\[
 ds^2=-dt^2+(dr+\sqrt{r_s/r}\,dt)^2+r^2d\Omega^2 .
\]
Writing $F=1-r_s/r$ and
\[
 T=t-2\sqrt{r_s r}
      -r_s\log\left|{\sqrt r-\sqrt{r_s}\over\sqrt r+\sqrt{r_s}}\right|
\]
gives $ds^2=-F\,dT^2+F^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2$.
A continuum rest ray follows $\dot r=v$ and hence
$\ddot r=vv'=-GE/r^2$ in the weak field: $G$ is defined by the Cavendish
measurement of this coefficient.  With $r=\varrho(1+r_s/(4\varrho))^2$
and $U=GE/\varrho$, the metric
has $g_{TT}=-1+2U-2U^2+\cdots$ and $g_{ij}=(1+2U+\cdots)\delta_{ij}$,
so $\beta=\gamma=1$.

Matter uses the actual adjoint-averaged site fields. One lattice correction
can be checked by freezing that effective flow to $-u_*$ with $N=1$.
On a lattice axis, a massless outward mode with $0<ak\ll1$ has
\[
 \omega={\sin(ak)\over a}-u_*\,{\sin(2ak)\over2a},\qquad
 \dot r=\cos(ak)-u_*\cos(2ak).
\]
At $u_*=1$ its speed is
$\tfrac32(ak)^2+O((ak)^4)$.
The identification of this local flow with $r=r_s$ belongs to the point
control. Finite-$\ell$ back-averaging and endpoint/packet corrections must
be retained for an actual exterior ray; no universal trapping shift is inferred.
In lattice units $r_s/a=2g\mathcal E$, with $g=G/a^2$ and
$\mathcal E=aE$; no value of the spacing is selected by this example.
