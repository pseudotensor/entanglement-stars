\noindent\textbf{Exterior lattice corrections.}
At fixed charge $E$, vacuum gives $N=1$, $v=-\sqrt{r_s/r}$,
$r_s=2GE$ with Cavendish $G$. The metric has $\beta=\gamma=1$,
marginal radius $r_s$ and null circle $3r_s/2$ when these lie in vacuum,
independently of $a,\ell$.
For the point principal symbol, uniformly averaging the apsidal orientation
in a plane of unit normal $\widehat L$ gives
\[
 B_L={3\over8}\left(1+\sum_i\widehat L_i^4\right),\qquad
 {\langle\Delta\varpi\rangle\over6\pi GE/[A(1-e^2)]}
 =1-{4B_L\over3}(am)^2+\cdots .
\]
The spacing coefficients are $-1,-2/3,-3/4$ for $[001],[111],[011]$;
the last is an orientation average, not a fixed-apsis law.
For massless rays match frequency $\omega$, group direction $d$ and impact
$b e_b$, with unit $d\perp e_b$;
put $A_4=\sum_i d_i^4$, $C_{22}=\sum_i d_i^2e_{b,i}^2$. Then
\[
 {\alpha_a\over4GE/b}
 =1-\left({A_4\over2}+2C_{22}\right)(a\omega)^2+\cdots,
 \qquad {T_{\log,a}\over T_{\log,0}}=1-3A_4(a\omega)^2+\cdots .
\]
Here $T_{\log}$ is the same-frequency flat-subtracted Shapiro logarithm
in static time or a round trip. Require weak fields, small spacing,
geometrical optics and bound eccentricity away from unity.
The axial signed leading bending factor is exactly
$(1-3\lambda/2)/(1-\lambda)$, $\lambda=(a\omega)^2<1$.
The point-symbol $xy$ periodic light ray has
$\overline r/(3r_s/2)=1-57(a\omega)^2/4+\cdots$; the positive-frequency
axial outward branch has no local turning point. Neither defines a universal
packet trapping radius. Finite-$\ell$ matter uses adjoint fields;
averaging, endpoint-ordering and band/packet corrections remain separate.
