\noindent\textbf{[R] Surface asymptotics and what is being measured.}

$\chi=a/r_s$.  In the declared continuum exterior the flat-slice clock is $N=1$
and $v=-w=-\sqrt{r_s/r}$.  The vanishing surface quantity is the Killing
lapse $\alpha=\sqrt{1-r_s/R}$, not $N$.  For radial infall from rest at
infinity, with proper time equal to the flat-slice time $t$,
\[
 \frac{dR}{dt}=-w,\qquad
 \frac{dR}{dT}=-w\alpha^2,\qquad
 \frac{d\alpha}{dT}=-\frac{\alpha}{2r_s}(1-\alpha^2)^{5/2},
\]
where $T=t-\int w\,dr/(1-w^2)$ is Schwarzschild time.
Hence $R-r_s\propto e^{-T/r_s}$ and
$\alpha\propto e^{-T/(2r_s)}$.  At observer retarded time
$u=T-r_*$, $dr_*/dr=(1-w^2)^{-1}$, instead
$R-r_s\propto e^{-u/(2r_s)}$.  \textbf{[X]} A regular incoming affine ray coordinate
therefore has $U=U_H-Ce^{-\kappa u}+\cdots$, $\kappa=1/(2r_s)$.
These are exterior consequences for this infalling branch, not a proof
that every matter preparation follows it.
